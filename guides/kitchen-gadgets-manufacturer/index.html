Kitchen Gadgets & Kitchen Accessories Manufacturer

Yongli is the factory behind many kitchen gadget programmes: utensil sets, scissors, can openers, strainers, measuring tools, mixing and prep gadgets.

Email a Quote RequestWhatsApp Us

Kitchen gadgets are rarely bought one at a time, so the practical question is how many items one factory can tool and pack together. Ours covers silicone and wooden-handled utensil sets, stainless steel scissors and shears, can openers, spiral and rotary graters, measuring cups and spoons, mixing bowls and prep accessories.

That breadth is also the cheapest way to build a private-label range: one colour system, one logo treatment, one carton language, applied across a dozen SKUs. We keep the colour and material spec on file so repeat orders match the first shipment.

Questions buyers ask

Can we build a whole range with one colour system?
Yes - that is the usual shape of a private-label programme. We hold the approved colour and material spec so every SKU in the range matches.
How many items can we mix in one order?
As many as you need, within one carton spec. Each item is quoted separately and then combined into a single production and inspection plan.
Do you make both silicone and stainless versions of the same gadget?
Often yes. A turner, for example, exists in all-silicone, silicone with a wooden handle, and stainless with a silicone head - three price points from one product idea.
Which certifications apply?
The factory is BSCI and ISO 9001 audited; food-contact materials are produced to FDA and LFGB requirements, with EN71 for children's items and REACH/RoHS where relevant.
