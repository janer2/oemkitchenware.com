Buyer guide

Coffee & Tea Accessories Manufacturer

Milk frothers, tea infusers, pour-over drippers, matcha sets and coffee tools - from a simple stainless ball infuser to an electric frother with CE/RoHS paperwork.

Email a Quote RequestWhatsApp Us

Coffee and tea accessories split into two production worlds: the purely mechanical items - infusers, drippers, strainers, measuring scoops, stands - and the electrical ones such as milk frothers, where CE and RoHS compliance and battery or mains configuration become part of the brief.

We produce both, which means a coffee programme can be quoted as one order: the frother as the hero SKU, the infuser, scoop and stand as the accessory items sharing its colour and finish, all packed under one retail design.

Customisation & quality

  • Custom mould and tooling design, with samples before mass production
  • Pantone colour matching and food-grade silicone hardness tuning
  • Logo printing, debossing and fully custom retail packaging
  • Material options tested to FDA, LFGB, EN71 and REACH
  • BSCI and ISO 9001 audited factory with in-process and pre-shipment inspection
  • One-stop OEM/ODM, packaging, design and logistics support

Questions buyers ask

Can you supply the electrical certification?
Electrical items are produced to CE and RoHS requirements with the paperwork that applies to the destination market confirmed before production.
Is the frother available with our own branding?
Yes - logo printing on the body and stand, plus printed packaging and manuals under your brand.
Do you supply mechanical accessories only?
Yes. Infusers, drippers, strainers and scoops are quoted on their own; many buyers start there before adding an electrical SKU.
Can the accessory items match the frother finish?
Yes, that is the point of quoting the range together: one colour and finish system across the hero product and its accessories.
