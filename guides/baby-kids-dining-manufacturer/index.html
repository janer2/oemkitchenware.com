Baby & Kids Dining Manufacturer

Divided plates with suction bases, bowls, spoons and forks, bibs, placemats and toddler snack cups - the feeding set a kids-dining range is built around.

Email a Quote RequestWhatsApp Us

Children's tableware is the most demanding thing we mould: the silicone has to be soft enough not to hurt, firm enough for high-chair suction to hold, and compliant with EN71 as well as the food-contact rules. We produce divided plates, suction bowls, spoons and forks, bibs with catch pockets, full-coverage placemats and snack cups.

Sets are usually where the value sits - a plate, bowl, spoon and bib sharing one colour and one animal or shape motif. We tool the motif into the mould so it prints in relief rather than being painted on, which is what keeps a children's range consistent across SKUs and safe through washing.

Questions buyers ask

Which safety standards apply to children's tableware?
EN71 for toy-safety aspects plus FDA and LFGB for food contact. Test reports are issued against the specific order.
Can we have our own character or shape?
Yes. Shapes and motifs are tooled into the mould, so they survive washing. Send the artwork and we will confirm tooling cost and sample timing.
Do you make the stainless steel kids plates?
Yes - stainless plate with removable silicone sleeve is one of our listed items.
Can one order cover a full feeding set?
Yes. Plate, bowl, cutlery, bib and placemat can be quoted as a set and packed in one retail carton.
