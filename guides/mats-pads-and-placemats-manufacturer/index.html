Silicone Mats, Pads & Placemats Manufacturer

Silicone placemats, table mats, drying mats and high-chair mats - flat goods where the printed surface, edge finish and thickness carry the whole design.

Email a Quote RequestWhatsApp Us

Mats are the simplest silicone product to make and the hardest to make well: the edge has to stay flat after washing, the printed decoration has to survive heat, and the thickness has to feel deliberate rather than thin. We mould them in house and control all three, from a plain table mat to a full-coverage high-chair mat with a printed pattern.

The neighbouring lines are the ones retailers usually order with them: dish drying mats in absorbent diatomaceous stone, baking and oven mats, fridge and drawer liners, and coaster sets in matching colours.

Questions buyers ask

Will the printed pattern survive washing?
Decoration is applied so it withstands normal washing and heat. The exact method is confirmed on the sample for your intended use.
Can you make an odd size for our table?
Yes, mats are cut from moulds we tool ourselves, so a non-standard size is a tooling job rather than a limitation.
What thicknesses are available?
Several, from thin placemat stock to thicker high-chair mats. We recommend one for the use case and you approve it on the sample.
Do you supply them with retail packaging?
Yes - printed sleeve, hang tag, roll or flat pack, agreed during quoting.
