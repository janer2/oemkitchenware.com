Buyer guide

BBQ & Grill Accessories Manufacturer

BBQ tool sets, spatulas, tongs, basting brushes, heat-resistant gloves and prep tubs - grill accessories usually sold as a boxed set, which is how we make them.

Email a Quote RequestWhatsApp Us

Grill accessories sell as a gift-ready set far more often than as loose tools, so the set logic - how many pieces, which ones, and how they stack in the case - is part of the brief. We make the stainless steel tools, the heat-resistant silicone heads and brushes, the gloves and the folding prep tub that doubles as a cutting board.

Because the same factory runs the kitchen tooling, a BBQ set can borrow handles, finishes and colourways already in production, which shortens tooling time and keeps a spring/summer range consistent with the rest of the catalogue.

Questions buyers ask

Can we choose the exact set contents?
Yes. Tell us the piece count and which tools, and we build the set, its case and its carton around that composition.
How heat resistant are the silicone parts?
Heat resistance depends on the grade specified for the item. We propose a grade for the intended grill use and confirm it on the sample.
Do you produce the seasonal volumes in time?
Spring and summer ranges are planned with the production and shipping calendar in advance - share your in-store date and we build the schedule backwards from it.
Is gift packaging possible?
Yes - printed carry case, colour box or insert card, which is the usual format for boxed grill sets.
