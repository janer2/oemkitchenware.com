Buyer guide

Lunch Box & Food Storage Manufacturer

Collapsible silicone lunch boxes, bento sets, snack boxes and food storage containers - leak-resistant, microwave and dishwasher safe, stackable for retail.

Email a Quote RequestWhatsApp Us

A lunch box is judged twice: at the till, where the silhouette and colour do the selling, and at home, where the lid seal and the ability to collapse decide whether it gets used again. We make the silicone-bodied, telescopic lunch box that folds down after the meal, and the rigid container with a clip or press lid for buyers who want a stacked, snap-together set.

Storage containers sold as a family - three or four sizes in one carton - are also a core line, in food-grade silicone and BPA-free plastic, with printed lids and colour-coded sizing for shelf impact.

Questions buyers ask

Are the collapsible boxes leak proof?
Leak resistance is a function of the lid and seal design. We quote the seal option that matches the use case and confirm it on the approved sample.
Can the whole box be printed with our design?
Yes - lid printing, body printing, or both, plus a printed sleeve or insert card for the retail carton.
Do you supply mixed sizes in one order?
Yes, container families are quoted per size and produced together so the carton mix is exactly what you plan to sell.
Which materials do you use?
Food-grade silicone and BPA-free plastic, produced to FDA and LFGB requirements, with test reports available for the order.
