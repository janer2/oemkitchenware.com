Collapsible & Foldable Storage Manufacturer

Collapsible food containers, folding bowls and colanders, telescopic cups and space-saving kitchen storage - products designed around the shipping volume they save.

Email a Quote Request WhatsApp Us See it on Alibaba.com

Collapsible goods are a logistics decision as much as a product one: a bowl that folds flat ships in a fraction of the carton, which changes the landed cost of an entire container load. We mould the silicone body and the rigid rim separately and assemble them so the fold line keeps its memory through repeated use.

The family covers food storage containers and lunch boxes, folding bowls and colanders, collapsible travel and camping cups, folding cutting boards that turn into a tub, and drawer and cabinet organisers for the storage side of the same retailer.

Questions buyers ask

How much shipping volume does a collapsible design save?
It depends on the fold ratio of the specific item. We give the flat-packed carton dimensions at quoting stage so you can compare landed cost directly.
Does the fold line stay strong after many uses?
The body is moulded for a long fold life and the rim is a separate rigid part. We confirm fold performance on the approved sample.
Can you supply the whole storage range in one order?
Yes - containers, bowls, cups and organisers are quoted item by item and produced together under one colour and packaging spec.
Are these items food safe?
Food-contact parts are food-grade silicone and BPA-free plastic, produced to FDA and LFGB requirements.
