Questions buyers ask

Which material should we choose for a garlic press?
Die-cast zinc gives the solid feel buyers associate with a premium press; stainless steel suits a dishwasher-led, rust-resistance message. We quote both and you compare.
Can the press be dishwasher safe?
Yes, the stainless steel and zinc versions are produced to dishwasher-safe finishing. We confirm the exact specification on the approved sample.
Do you offer custom colour or coating?
Yes - colour coating matching a Pantone reference, with the sample approved before the production run.
What is the pack-out like?
Printed card, blister, colour box or polybag with label, decided with you at quoting stage so the carton dimensions are agreed early.
