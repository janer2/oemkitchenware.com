Bread Proofing Basket Manufacturer (Banneton & Dough Boxes)

We produce banneton proofing baskets, linen liners, collapsible proofing bowls and stackable dough proofing boxes - the baker's-tool side of a sourdough programme.

Email a Quote RequestWhatsApp Us

Proofing baskets are sold in sets far more often than individually: a basket with its cloth liner and often a scraper, lame, whisk or dough whisk alongside it. Our tooling covers the round and oval shapes, the coiled rattan look and the collapsible silicone or plastic bowls that fold flat for shipping - whichever the retail price point calls for.

For brands that sell a dough proofing box rather than a basket, we make airtight-lid containers in food-grade silicone and BPA-free plastic, sized to standard pizza and loaf doughs and stackable for retail shelving.

Questions buyers ask

Which shapes and sizes can you tool?
Round from 5 in to 10 in, oval, and custom sizes. Send a drawing or a sample and we will quote tooling and unit price for that exact shape.
Do you supply the linen liner as well?
Yes - liners are cut and sewn to fit the basket, printed with your logo if you want, and floured-ready when the buyer opens the box.
Are the baskets food safe?
They are made from food-contact materials and produced to FDA and LFGB requirements. Test reports can be issued with the shipment.
Can we mix several shapes in one order?
Yes. Mixed configurations are normal for our export customers; the quote is built per shape, then combined into one production and shipping plan.
