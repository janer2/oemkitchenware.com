2. What we collect

2.1 When you send an enquiry

Our enquiry form asks for your name, email address, and optionally your phone or WhatsApp number, company name, country and message. We use this only to answer your enquiry and to prepare a quotation.

2.3 What we do not collect

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, fingerprinting scripts or social media pixels. We do not buy contact lists and we do not sell or rent data to anyone.

3. Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies. The session identifier described above is kept in your browser’s sessionStorage, which is deleted automatically when you close the tab. Choosing “decline” is therefore not applicable, and no consent banner is shown.

Some providers are based outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the United States. Where that happens, transfers rely on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

7. How long we keep it

  • Anonymous click events: 90 days, then deleted.

  • Quotations, orders and invoices: as long as tax and commercial law requires.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to confirm what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, and receive it in a portable format. Write to info@yonglicc.com and we will answer within one month.

If you believe we have handled your data unlawfully, you may complain to your national data protection authority. If you are in California, you may also exercise the rights granted by the CCPA/CPRA; we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined there.

9. Children

This is a business-to-business site. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

10. Changes
