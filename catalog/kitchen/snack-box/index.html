Range Highlights

Practical food and snack containers for lunchboxes, kids' meals and on-the-go eating — designed to stay leak-resistant through a school day or a commute, and to stack neatly in the fridge or pantry.

  • BPA-free, food-safe materials; kid-friendly rounded corners
  • Airtight, leak-resistant seals to keep snacks and lunch fresh
  • Stackable, space-saving designs for lunch bags and refrigerators
  • Portion-friendly sizes from small snack cups to full lunch boxes
  • OEM / ODM: custom colors, molds, silicone seal colors and retail packaging
