Range Highlights

Food-grade silicone feeding essentials for babies and toddlers — weaning sets, bibs, cups and utensils designed with soft edges, gentle textures and simple shapes that parents can trust.

  • Ultra-soft food-grade silicone, gentle on tender gums and developing teeth
  • One-piece, crevice-free designs that are effortless to rinse and sterilize
  • BPA-free materials; referenced to EN71 and FDA food-contact expectations
  • Suction-base options help keep bowls and plates in place at mealtime
  • Custom pastel colors, character-free brand embossing and gift-ready packaging
