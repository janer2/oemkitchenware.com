Development History

2010 — Set Sail

Yongli founded on Jan 7th, 2010. BSCI verified manufacturer for kitchen ware, table wares, outdoor and packaging products.

2012 — Move

Marketing center completed. Expanded OEM service and sales team, working with 40 suppliers in a month.

2013 — OEM

8-year experienced R&D team developed over 10 SKUs for packing products, winning orders from NUXE, O.P.I and more.

2014 — Expand

Added silicone kitchen ware product line; worked with K-Mart and Target with great feedback.

2016–2019 — Opportunity

Global market network; cooperated with Amazon top sellers; expanded stainless steel kitchen wares line.

2019 — International Exhibition

Chicago Housewares Show (US) and Birmingham Gift Show (UK), building firm global relationships.

2021 — Win

Honored with the certificate of advanced collective; senior staff recognized as national model worker.

2022 — A Reliable Partner

One-stop OEM, packaging, design and logistics service, keeping our vision: Make Globe Trade Easy.
